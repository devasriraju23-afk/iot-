Actions

Unlock Door

Grants authorized access. Auto-relock activates after the configured timeout.

Lock Door

Manually engages the simulated lock. Cancels any pending auto-relock timer.

Open Door

Simulates the door being physically pushed open. Only works when unlocked.

Close Door

Simulates the door being physically closed (not locked).

Simulate Unauthorized Attempt

Simulates an intruder trying to access the door without authorization. Triggers the security alarm.

Dismiss Alarm

Manually clears the active security alarm. Also auto-dismisses after 30 seconds.
